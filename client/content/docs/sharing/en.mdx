Everyone on a team already has every task in it. Sharing is for someone outside the team: it gives them one task and nothing else of the team's. To give someone everything, an administrator invites them to the team instead.

## Share a task

Open the task, press Share, and give three things: the person's email address, what they can do, and for how long.

| They can | What that means |
| --- | --- |
| Watch | Read the conversation and watch the desktop's screen. Nothing they send reaches the agent or the desktop. |
| Watch and write to the agent | The same, and send messages in the conversation. |
| Watch, write and use the desktop | The same, and use the mouse and keyboard on the desktop. |

The share lasts an hour, a day, a week, or until you take it back.

## What the other person gets

If the address already has a Workstream account, that person has the task at once, and Workstream emails them a link to it.

If it does not, Workstream emails them a link to create an account with that address, and the task opens when they have. The link works once, only for that address, and for a week, or until the share would end if that is sooner.

The task appears under Shared with me in their sidebar and opens in their own workspace, beside their own team's tasks.

## Who has a task, and taking it back

The Share dialog lists everyone who has the task and until when. Anyone on the team can take a share back there, or withdraw an invitation that has not been accepted.

A share that has ended or been taken back stops working at the person's next request. A desktop they are watching at that moment closes within a minute.

## What sharing gives away

- Someone who can write to the agent can ask it to do anything the task's desktop can do. If the task's environment has a repository, that includes pushing a branch to it and opening a pull request.
- Someone who can use the desktop can read everything on it, including the [environment's variables](/docs/environments#variables).
- Someone who can only watch sees the screen and the conversation, so they see anything shown there.
